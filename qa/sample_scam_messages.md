# Sample Scam Messages — Manual QA

> Use these with `POST /analyze/text` and `POST /analyze/investigation`.
> Expected result: `is_scam: true` (or high confidence) for each.

---

## Bank / KYC Scams

1. `URGENT: Your SBI account will be deactivated in 24 hours. Update KYC immediately: https://sbi-kyc.xyz`
2. `Aadhaar KYC expiry: Your Aadhaar-linked bank account will be frozen. Update now: https://aadhaar-verify.cf`
3. `ICICI Bank Alert: Your debit card has been blocked due to suspicious activity. Call 1800-XXX-XXXX immediately.`
4. `Your HDFC account is temporarily locked. Click https://hdfc-secure.tk to unlock within 12 hours.`
5. `Axis Bank: Your netbanking password has expired. Re-verify: https://axis-bank-verify.top`

## UPI / Payment Scams

6. `Your UPI transaction of Rs 15000 is pending. Confirm now to avoid penalty: https://paytm-upi.tk`
7. `Scan the QR code below to receive Rs 5000 cashback from PhonePe.`
8. `GPay: Your payment of Rs 25000 failed. Update your UPI PIN: https://gpay-verify.xyz`
9. `PhonePe KYC link: Your account will be limited if not verified. https://phonepe-kyc.cf`

## Lottery / Prize Scams

10. `Congratulations! You won 50 Lakh in our mega lottery. Call +91-9876543210 now to claim your prize!`
11. `You have been selected for the Amazon Lucky Draw. Claim your Rs 25000 gift card: https://amazon-winner.tk`
12. `Your mobile number won a free iPhone 16. Pay Rs 499 shipping to claim: https://iphone-winner.xyz`

## Job / Investment Scams

13. `Work from home job - earn 50000/month. Registration fee Rs 500 required.`
14. `Double your investment in 30 days! Guaranteed returns. Contact: telegram @invest_king`
15. `Bitcoin investment! Turn Rs 10000 into 1 Lakh in one week. https://bitcoin-profit.cf`
16. `Personal loan approved! 10 Lakh at 2% interest. Pay Rs 2000 processing fee first.`

## Courier / Customs Scams

17. `Your courier parcel from Dubai is held at customs. Pay Rs 5000 release fee to clear it.`
18. `Customs seized your international parcel. Pay Rs 25000 clearance fee immediately.`
19. `FedEx: Your package is stuck due to wrong address. Update details: https://fedex-update.tk`

## Government / Subsidy Scams

20. `PM Modi's new subsidy scheme: Get Rs 50000 in your account. Register: https://pm-subsidy.cf`
21. `Your electricity bill is overdue. Disconnection in 24 hours. Pay via: https://tneb-payment.top`
22. `Income Tax refund of Rs 12000 is pending. Claim: https://incometax-refund.xyz`

## Urgency / Fear-Based Scams

23. `Your account has been compromised! Call our customer care at 1800-123-4567 immediately.`
24. `FINAL NOTICE: Legal action will be taken if you do not pay Rs 5000 within 24 hours.`
25. `Your SIM will be deactivated in 2 hours. Re-verify now: https://sim-verify.tk`

## OTP / Credential Theft

26. `Your Amazon account has been logged in from a new device. Share the OTP 482916 to confirm.`
27. `Netflix: Your payment failed. Verify card details to continue service: https://netflix-billing.cf`

## Social Engineering / Fake Support

28. `Hello sir, I am calling from your bank. Please share your OTP to complete the KYC.`
29. `Your WhatsApp account will be suspended. Verify: https://whatsapp-verify.top`
30. `Dear customer, your PAN card has been linked to illegal transactions. Call 1800-XXX immediately to resolve.`
